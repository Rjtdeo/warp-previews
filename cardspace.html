The dead space inside the cards

Same four rates every time. Only what the card does with its vertical space changes.

Where it came from: to stop one tier sitting taller than the next I put a 286px floor on every card. That fixed the rows and guaranteed a void inside any card with fewer lines than the tallest one — the gap you are seeing is the floor, doing exactly what it was told. Every option below removes the floor and evens the cards up by giving them the same content instead.
