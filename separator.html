Separating the two chip groups

Same chips every time. Only the thing between DHL and Any service changes.

Why the green dot reads oddly: on this row green already means selected — it is the state of “All carriers” and “Any service”. A green dot beside them is the same colour doing a second, unrelated job, so the eye tries to read it as another chip or a status marker before it settles on punctuation. Whatever separates the groups is better off in a colour that is not carrying meaning three chips away.
