Four directions for the rate results

The same twenty-odd carrier rates, drawn five ways. “Today” is the page as it ships now; V1–V4 are whole answers to the same question rather than variations on one layout. Switch the lane to test each against density and against collapse.

Lane

What the redesign is actually fixing

None of the four layouts is the real finding. The finding is that two fields which decide the purchase are already in the quote payload and are thrown away before they reach the screen.

estimated_delivery_date

The carrier already told us the date. The page prints a day count instead.

Today every card reads “3 days transit.” Shipping Wednesday 2 September, three business days is Tuesday the 8th — six calendar days away, because a weekend and Labor Day sit inside the window. A day count cannot say that. A date says it without being asked.

guaranteed_service

Whether the carrier commits to a time, with money back if it slips.

It is most of what the express tier sells, and it never reaches the screen. It is also a load-bearing third axis: ranking on price and speed alone deletes the entire express tier as redundant, which on a lane where somebody needs a 10:30 commitment is the most expensive mistake this page could make.

dominance

Most of what is on screen is a choice nobody would defend.

An option that costs more while arriving no sooner and guaranteeing no more is one nothing would pick. On LA→Chicago that is fourteen of twenty options; on LA→San Diego, twenty of twenty-three. Today all of them compete for attention at equal weight, and the customer does the elimination by hand, nine cards at a time.

Recommendation: V3, arrival-first. It is the only one whose structure is the question the customer actually has — when does it land, and what is the cheapest way to hit that day. Collapsed, it answers both in one screen. The caveat worth knowing: V2 hides legitimate options — on the metro lane Sway sits on the frontier but wins no superlative, so a cheapest/fastest/best-value layout drops it entirely.
